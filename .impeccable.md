## Design Context

### Users
HR staff and managers use HRM to scan employee records and complete daily people operations. On the documents page, they need to find, upload, download, and control access to employee files across the company. Some accounts have view access to individually shared documents; document managers can manage all documents.

### Brand Personality
Professional, calm, and clear. The interface should support confident daily work without drawing attention away from the records.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the HRM design system: white-first, compact, data-led layouts with semantic colors, blue for primary actions, and both light and dark themes. Keep Thai labels direct and controls responsive.

### Design Principles
- Put document search and filters next to the list they affect.
- Keep employee identity, file details, and actions easy to scan.
- Preserve server-enforced user and per-document permissions.
- Reuse shared HRM components and semantic theme tokens.
- Make list and upload workflows keyboard-accessible and usable on narrow screens.
